# Calculator: Addition

1. Generate two random numbers from 1 to 10 using `echo "{\"num1\": $((RANDOM % 10 + 1)), \"num2\": $((RANDOM % 10 + 1))}"` command.
2. Sum them in the application.
3. Check that the result is mathematically correct.
